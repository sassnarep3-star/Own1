What the source says about the prize

Verified on TradingView's official rules page: total cash ARV $50,000, paid via wire transfer or PayPal (≥$1,000) or PayPal only (<$1,000); places 51–250 receive a 3-month subscription. Free to enter with a $100,000 virtual paper-trading account (10:1 leverage, $0.85/contract on futures). The Leap runs periodic editions — the July 2026 TradeStation edition closed 14 Aug; new editions launch regularly, so check the live page for the next window.
