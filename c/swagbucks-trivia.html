What the source says about the prize

Direct quote from the ledger dataset. Unconfirmed unless the row is marked live-checked.

Verified on Swagbucks' own help pages and app listing (Prodege LLC): Daily Trivia Live runs Monday–Thursday with a shared prize pot, and the hostless Daily Trivia Challenge runs every day. Winnings are paid in SB points, redeemed for PayPal cash or gift cards at swagbucks.com/redeem. Honest framing: per-game wins are typically cents (e.g. $0.10–$0.50), not the '$1000s every week' headline, which describes the pot shared across the whole player base.
