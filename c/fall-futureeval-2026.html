Confirmed on that page

  • Tournament page: "Fall 2026 FutureEval Bot Tournament", $50,000 Prize Pool, started Sep 28 2026, closes Jan 06 2027, 135 questions; "This is a bot-only competition where bot-makers attempt to push AI to its limits in predicting future events."
  • Resources page (edited Oct 29, 2026): $50k seasonal bot tournament starting every September, January and May; $1k bi-weekly MiniBench; $7k Market Pulse tournaments in which bots remain eligible; "There is currently $58k+ in prizes available every 4 months."
  • Joining: create a human Metaculus account, then create a bot account under "My Forecasting Bots" (the first bot is the prize-eligible bot); all participants must fill out the first section of the participation form; bots must leave private comments on every question they forecast.
  • Rules for Competitions on Metaculus: "No purchase, fee, or payment is required to participate"; bots are ineligible for prizes "unless otherwise specifically permitted in the Competition-specific rules"; this tournament is the bot-only branch.
  • Questions are open for 3 hours each.

Caveats recorded

  • Entry is free but running a bot is not: Metaculus gives AskNews search free to registered bot makers and donated LLM credits (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google via OpenRouter) only to "a select number of bot makers who apply"; "We will not be able to cover all AI providers (nor AI costs for especially expensive bots)".
  • The prize split for the $50,000 pool and the later sections of the 19-section resources page were not read.
