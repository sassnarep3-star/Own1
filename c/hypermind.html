What the source says about the prize

Direct quote from the ledger dataset. Unconfirmed unless the row is marked live-checked.

Verified on dash.hypermind.com: Hypermind (since 2014, run by Lumenogic — the team behind the Good Judgment Project's IARPA win) is 'free to play, with cash rewards.' Everyone starts with 200,000 play-money 'Hypermoney'; contest rewards are split pro-rata of relative forecasting performance, and earnings accumulate across contests, withdrawable as Amazon gift certificates (minimum $20 / €15). The dashboard showed 24,573€ at stake across live contests at review. No purchase necessary.
