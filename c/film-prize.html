What the source says about the prize

Direct quote from the ledger dataset. Unconfirmed unless the row is marked live-checked.

Verified on prizefest.com's official rules: the Louisiana Film Prize awards 'the largest cash prize for a short film in the world — $50,000.' The entry fee is $0. Films must be scripted live-action shorts of 5–15 minutes; principal photography (90% of runtime) must be in Louisiana — Caddo/Bossier Parish for $50,000, elsewhere in the state for $25,000. Each of the 20 finalists receives $250.

Verification the operator runs

  • Register the project (free) before shooting.
  • Finalists submit a W-9 before the check is issued.

Watch-outs (from the source)

  • Requires filming in Louisiana — a real production commitment, not a casual entry.
