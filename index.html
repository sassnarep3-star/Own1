Executive summary

The short version, with the reasoning in full on the sources page.

What this is
A verified-where-possible ledger of 211 free-to-enter cash-competition leads, rebuilt so every displayed fact has a quoted official source and every gap is visible.
What is solid
20 entries were checked line by line against the organiser's own pages on 2026-11-01. 13 have confirmed free entry and 16 a confirmed cash prize. Those records carry a fee status, a prize string, a deadline where one is published, and the exact quote behind each.
What is not
185 leads remain unverified name-and-link entries inherited from the reference catalog, plus 6 that could only be partly checked because their boards need JavaScript or sign-in. No prize, fee or deadline is asserted for them.
Best near-term moves
Codex x TwitchCon (closes 2026-11-02, AI assistants explicitly allowed), US Midterms 2026 on Metaculus (closes 2026-11-04, free, low effort) and the ARC Prize deadline (2026-11-02) — noting ARC has not published an entry fee and bans internet access during evaluation.
Biggest irregularities
A paid-entry poetry contest was listed as free; one trading link points at an edition that already closed; a crowdfunding queue was listed as a prize contest; Challenge.gov was sunset in March 2026; two leads have no operator link at all. Each is on the flagged page with its source.
How to use it
Start with the ranked shortlist, open the entry page, read the quoted rule, then decide. Never pay a fee to claim a prize, and expect a tax event in the US when you win.
