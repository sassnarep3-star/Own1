Categories

12 competitions · 8 active Skill tournaments

Head-to-head skill games — solitaire, bingo, blocks. The free rooms pay gems or tickets; the dollar prizes sit in cash rooms that charge an entry fee, and the house takes a rake.

 2 competitions · 1 active Trivia competitions

Quiz formats. This is the thinnest category in the ledger: one row is a closed contest and the other pays in points rather than dollars.

 129 competitions · 93 active Fantasy & sports picks

NFL, college and soccer pick'em, survivor, bracket and fantasy contests. The largest category and the most seasonal — check status before you invest a season.

 18 competitions · 17 active Creative contests

Writing, photo and judged submissions. Entry is usually free, but judging is slow and subjective, and deadlines decide whether a row is worth anything today.

 4 competitions · 3 active Forecasting tournaments

Scored probability forecasts against other people, with prizes funded by sponsors. Free to enter, objectively scored, and the one category where a bot tournament already exists.

 19 competitions · 19 active Market competitions

Paper-trading and simulated-investing contests. Free to enter, but many are broker customer-acquisition funnels and some prizes are credits or funded demo accounts, not cash.

 18 competitions · 18 active Tech & data competitions

Machine-learning competitions and hackathons with published prize pools. Free to enter, objectively scored, and the best structural fit for an AI-assisted entrant.

 2 competitions · 2 active Directories & forums

Third-party catalogues of contests. Useful for discovery only — every linked contest still needs its own rules check, and many listed contests charge entry fees.

 7 competitions · 7 active Free pool & league platforms

Free tools for running your own pool or league. Nothing is funded by the operator; the prize pot is your group's money.

How this site differs from a link dump

  • One row, one operator link. Every row has a link to the operator's own page and, where one exists, a separate rules link. No affiliate redirects.
  • Blank beats guessed. 85 rows show “—” for the prize because the source states no amount, cites no official source, or the official source no longer loads (I-23). We never fill in a number.
  • Status is visible. 27 ended contests are stamped and excluded from every shortlist instead of hiding in the scroll.
  • Badges mean something. “Live-checked” = we fetched a page of that row on 1 Nov 2026. “Source cited” = we did not.
  • Problems are published, not buried. 23 irregularities with evidence and links are on Flagged for review.
