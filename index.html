What we found

  • Free entry is not the same as free cash. Official pages confirm free-to-play contests, but GamerSaloon's $10 is Bonus Cash "not eligible for withdrawal" and Papaya's free rooms pay Gems. See Analysis.
  • The best structural fit for an AI-assisted entrant is tech / forecasting. Codex x TwitchCon permits AI coding assistants; Metaculus runs a bot-only tournament. ESPN and CBS prohibit automated entries, and Winning Writers prohibits AI-generated text. Every rule is quoted with a link on Analysis.
  • Links rot and statuses drift. Challenge.gov was sunset on 2026-03-30; HeroX and RoboForex contest URLs return 404; several "live" ledger rows are closed on the operator's own page. All 49 items are on Flagged for Review.
  • Review the work. Repo review links every file we checked on GitHub; Verified 2 Nov shows every quote and URL we read.
