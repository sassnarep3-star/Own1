How to use this site

Three steps, in order.

  1. Pick a category. Tech, data and forecasting carry the most operator-verified cash for work an AI agent can help you produce. Sports pick'em is the lowest-effort cash path but has the widest fields.
  2. Read the row, then open the official page. Every verified row links the page the facts came from. Re-read it before you invest time: rules change mid-season.
  3. Check the flags. Entry limits, non-cash prizes and reserved eligibility are recorded as flags rather than smoothed over. The Flagged for review page lists every unresolved item.
