## Important interpretation for AI-assisted entry

- The general Metaculus competition rules allow a human forecaster to use AI assistance only when the submitted forecasts reflect that person's own decisions and understanding. They generally bar bots from prizes unless a contest-specific exception applies. FutureEval is explicitly described as bot-only; read its contest-specific guidance before implementing or deploying a bot.
- The Codex x TwitchCon rules expressly require OpenAI Codex, a Twitch-connected project, a repository, a public demo video and a session ID. The listed first-place amount is not the whole prize pool; the official prize schedule includes five awards totalling the event's `$25,000` headline.
- The Global Virtual Hackathon explicitly allows and expects AI tools, but it is a youth/student team competition, not an unrestricted individual contest.
- A large advertised prize is not a win estimate. No odds, expected return, tax treatment, or jurisdiction-specific legal conclusion is calculated in this repository.
