Method & verification log

Rules applied

  • Source of rows: the 206 listings in the upstream ledger (live site, commit 466f7c1). They are treated as claims, not facts.
  • Verified means: a human-readable official page (operator, league, sponsor or platform domain) was fetched on 2026-11-01 and states the value shown. Nothing is inferred or filled in.
  • Blank = unverified. If a value could not be read from an official page, the “Verified prize” cell is left empty.
  • Upstream columns (status, entry, region) are labelled “upstream/auto” and kept visible so you can see the claim, but they are not verification.
  • “Region” and “prize type” are automatic keyword matches on upstream wording — handy filters, not facts.

Verification levels

VerifiedPrize and key terms read from an official page on 2026-11-01. (6)
Partly verifiedOfficial page loads and confirms some values; others could not be confirmed. (9)
Differs from officialOfficial page contradicts or changes the upstream claim (closed, stale year, different amount). (7)
Broken linkUpstream link returns an error page. (1)
Not confirmedPage opened but no usable prize/terms text. (5)
Not checkedNot opened in this session. (178)

Limitations (read this)

  • Pages were read through a text fetcher, not a full browser: JavaScript-rendered listings (e.g. Zindi) and login-walled pages can't be read, and are reported as unverified.
  • Only 28 of 206 rows were opened against an official page. The rest are published with blank prize cells and the upstream claim tucked under “Details”. Re-run verification in a follow-up session; the board in COORDINATION.md lists it as unclaimed.
  • Outbound network access in the build environment is limited, so external links on the site cannot be machine-checked at build time; scripts/linkcheck.js validates every internal link and anchor, and external links were checked only where listed below.
  • Eligibility, tax forms and full rules were read only where stated in a row's Details.
