The rules this site follows

  • No unsourced numbers. A prize, fee or deadline appears only with a quote from the organiser's own page.
  • Blank beats wrong. If the official page does not state it, the cell stays empty and is flagged “not stated”.
  • A fee that is not stated is not zero. ARC Prize, Kaggle, DrivenData and GEMS show no fee on the pages read, so their fee cell is blank rather than “free”.
  • Contradictions are published, not resolved in the sponsor's favour. ESPN's two entry limits and PayPal's two prize totals are both shown.
  • Re-verification is required before action. Every fact carries the date it was read. Rules change; the quote is a snapshot.
  • The reference catalog is not a source. It supplied names and links only, and its August 2026 status labels are not reproduced as current facts.
