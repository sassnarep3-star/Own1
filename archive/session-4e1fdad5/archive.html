Parallel work kept, not deleted

Several agent sessions built dashboards against this repository at the same time. This is the final integration session: the main site was rebuilt from verified data, and the other sessions’ research artefacts and pages were kept exactly where they were so nothing was lost.

Why two sets of pages exist. The dashboard, category pages, shortlist, summary, method and review queue are generated by this repository’s build (scripts/build.mjs) from data/entries.json. The pages below come from a parallel session’s Python build and are preserved for their evidence trail, not as a second navigation path. Where the two disagree, this site’s verified data wins, and the disagreement is listed on the Flagged for review page.
