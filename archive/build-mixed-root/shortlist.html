Tiered shortlist

Where the evidence is strongest

Four tiers, assigned by rules rather than intuition. Tier 1 needs three verified facts: free entry, a cash prize, and a contest that was open on the verification date. Anything less falls a tier.

This is not a prediction of winnings. The order comes from a published actionability score that rewards complete, verified facts — free entry (+25), a printed cash prize (+20), open at check time (+15), a printed deadline (+10), printed eligibility (+10) and a stated low-effort format (+10). It says nothing about your odds. See the rubric.
