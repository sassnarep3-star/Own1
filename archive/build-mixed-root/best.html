Shortlists, not promises

Where your time is best spent

Four shortlists built only from fields in the dataset: entry path, official sourcing, live check, stated cash size, ID burden, platform and the flags we raised. Nothing here is a recommendation to gamble or to trust an operator. The score is printed on every row so you can audit it.

The scoring model, in full. Each row's score is printed under its name, term by term. Access terms: free entry +3 (some free events +1, cash needs a fee −4); official-tier source +2; live-checked on 1 Nov 2026 +2 (a link error −2). Cash terms: the dollar figure the claim commits to (the figure it leads with, or the low end of a stated range) scores $100k+ +4, $10k+ +3, $1k+ +2, under $1k +1; a claim that commits to no figure loses 1 — we never read a number out of "up to", "commonly", "varies" or a funded-account promise. Friction terms: account-only signup +2, eligibility check +1, full KYC −1, tax paperwork −2; browser entry +1. Category fit (our editorial judgement about whether one good entry can place, and whether the work suits an AI-assisted entrant): tech +3, forecasting +3, market +1, creative +1, sports 0, skill 0, pools −3. Penalties: broker/sportsbook account required −6, non-cash prize currency −2, rules link points at a past contest −2, a live check that found no open entry window −4. Ended and inactive rows are excluded outright. None of it is a claim about your odds.
