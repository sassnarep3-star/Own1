Method and sources

Rules applied to every row

  1. Every row links out. A row must carry a link to the operator’s own page. If none could be found, the row says so instead of substituting a news article or an HTTP-only page.
  2. Facts come from that page. Prize, entry cost, deadline and eligibility are copied from the linked page on the date recorded in the row’s evidence block. If the page does not state a value, the cell stays blank.
  3. Blank ≠ zero and blank ≠ free. Only an explicit statement such as "The Competition is free to enter" produces a "Free entry" badge.
  4. Nothing is inferred. No prize is rounded, converted, combined or extrapolated; no deadline is guessed from a season pattern; no probability is estimated.
  5. Disagreements are published. When an operator’s own page contradicts itself, both readings are recorded and the row is flagged rather than silently resolved.

Automated checks

node tests/validate.mjs runs on every push and verifies: unique ids; every category and evidence status valid; every unverified row has blank prize, entry, deadline, kind and eligibility; every checked row has an evidence block with a source URL and at least one confirmed value; every row’s source is HTTPS; every internal link and asset on all 13 pages resolves; and no table is forced wider than a 390px viewport.

Parallel evidence kept in this repository

  • Review page: the 28 row-by-row reads recorded in data/verification/batchA.jsonl, each with its outcome, prize text and official URL.
  • Archived method page from the parallel build, which explains its 90-point priority score.
  • Verified ledger build: all 206 imported rows with an automated audit and its own flagged page.
  • data/ledger.json, data/audit.json, data/verification.json, data/finder-ledger.json: the machine-readable evidence stores, unchanged.

Known limits of this pass

  • 184 rows were not read on an operator page and are published blank and flagged.
  • Prize and deadline values age quickly. The reading date is shown on every checked row.
  • Eligibility summaries are shortened from longer rules; the linked page wins in any dispute.
  • Pages that render only with JavaScript (Topcoder, Zindi, the Reedsy contest entries) could not be read reliably, so they stay unverified.
  • This is not legal, financial or tax advice. US federal tax reporting generally begins at $600 of prizes in a year, but eligibility and filing duties are personal.
