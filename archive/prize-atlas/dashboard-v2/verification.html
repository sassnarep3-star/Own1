Why only 12?

Because a number that was not read on an official page is a guess, and this project’s rule is that a guess is worse than a blank. Rows were prioritised by value: the rows where a real cash prize is claimed to be free, plus every row that a reasonable person would consider spending a weekend on. If you want a row promoted, the route is mechanical — open the operator page, record the URL, quote the text, re-run tools/verify_check.py.
