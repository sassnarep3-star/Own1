How to close an item

  1. Open the operator page linked in the row.
  2. Read prize, entry cost, deadline and eligibility; copy the wording, not your summary.
  3. Record the reading date, the exact facts copied and anything the page left unstated in an evidence block on the row.
  4. Only then set sourceStatus to checked and fill the cells you actually read.
