How to use this list

  • Closing in days, not weeks: ARC Prize (today, Paper Track 8 Nov), Codex x TwitchCon (today), Metaculus US Midterms (4 Nov).
  • Best fit for an AI coding agent, per the pages themselves: Google Quantum AI circuit compilation (its overview names AI coding agents), Gemma 4 Developer Agent, Squares in Squares, and the two Kaggle code competitions.
  • Tool-locked hackathons: Kiroween needs Kiro, Codegeist needs Atlassian Forge, Codex x TwitchCon needs OpenAI Codex.
  • Offline-only evaluation: ARC Prize, Name That LLM and Enveda CASMI forbid or disable internet access at evaluation, so the submitted solution cannot call a hosted model.
  • Before you enter anything: read the rules page linked in the Source column; eligibility and prize-payment terms (tax forms, licence of winning code) are in there, not on this site.
