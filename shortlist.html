Tier rules used on this page

Tier A

Start here. Free entry confirmed by the operator, cash prize of at least $5,000 stated by the operator, and a deliverable an AI coding agent can materially help produce (model, paper, app, or structured picks).

Tier B

Worth tracking. Verified free entry and cash, but smaller pools, reserved eligibility, long horizons, or a heavy production burden.

Tier C

Context only. Verified, but the prize is non-cash, the pool is missing, or the row is a platform rather than one event.

Tiers are editorial triage, not a measured expected value. No source inspected publishes win probabilities, so no expected-value figure appears anywhere on this site.
