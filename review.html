What would clear an item

  • Read the organiser's own rules page and record the exact quote for the field being claimed.
  • Record the date read, because all of these facts expire.
  • If the page will not render without JavaScript or sign-in, say so and leave the field blank.
  • If two official pages disagree, publish both and flag the contradiction rather than picking the more attractive number.
