## Method notes

- Pages were fetched once, on the verification date, with a text reader. Nothing was recalled from
  memory and no value was copied from an aggregator. Where a reader returned only a JavaScript
  shell, the row was marked unverified instead of filled in.
- Currency and dates are recorded exactly as the operator writes them. Deadlines are stored as
  `YYYY-MM-DD` only when the page gives a date; otherwise the wording goes in `deadlineNote`.
- Every verified row also records `verifiedOn`. A verified row with no date would be a bug; the
  test suite enforces this.
