Reviewed 2026-11-01 UTC. This is a focused review, not an exhaustive verification of the catalog or a finding of eligibility for any particular person. The [competition homepage](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/) points to a [rules page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/rules/), which points through [HeroX](https://www.herox.com/GEMSPrize/resource/2274) to the [September 2026 official rules](https://www.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf). Read current official materials before acting.

## Other irregularities for review

- The rules' §1.4 includes program-goal language requiring content that confirms an intent to commercialize early-stage technology and establish a viable U.S.-based business. It is not obvious how this relates to the competition's algorithm-prediction format; ask the organizer before relying on an interpretation. [Official rules §1.4](https://www.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf).
- Section 3.2 requires a generative-AI narrative, whereas §3.5 describes predictions, code and documentation without an obvious narrative submission field. Confirm how and when to make the disclosure. [Official rules §§3.2, 3.5](https://www.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf).
- Entry fees, all eligibility restrictions, source-code licensing obligations and the actual time investment are not completely verified here. Do not infer them from silence.
